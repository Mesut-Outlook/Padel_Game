During a Match

Movement

W A S DMove: W toward the net, S back, A left, D right
↑ ← ↓ →Arrow keys work the same way

Smart Shot

Space or JSmart shot: automatically selects drive, volley, bandeja, or smash
A / D or ← / → + shotAim left / right (hold direction while swinging)

Special Shots

KLob (globo): high and deep over the opponents
LSmash: powerful overhead strike on high balls
UChiquita: soft, low ball dropping just over the net
IVíbora: side-spin slicing overhead that skids low
OContrapared: blast off your own glass wall over the net (hold A/D for side glass)

Serve

WASD (before serving)Slide along the baseline inside your half behind the service line
SpaceServe: toss, bounce, and underhand strike into the diagonal box
FSlice serve: stays low and curves sideways
A / D or ← / → + serveAim left or right inside the service box

Pro Tips

Swing earlyPress the shot key slightly before the ball arrives — your player strikes at optimal reach.
Plant your feetOnce you arm a swing, your feet plant so aiming left/right won't pull you away from the ball.
Off the glassDon't rush balls heading to the back glass — let them rebound first, then strike.

Two players on one PC: set Human players to 2. Player 1 plays on the keyboard, player 2 on the first Xbox controller (more controllers = players 3 and 4). In singles you play against each other; in doubles choose Opponents (each of you gets an AI partner) or Same team (you two against 2 AI).

Tablet & Phone (iPad, Android)

Turn the device sideways: the game plays in landscape. A tablet is the most comfortable; a phone works too, but the court is small.

Getting started on iPad / iPhone

  1. Open padellegends.eu in Safari or Chrome.
  2. For full screen: tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then start the game from the new icon. It opens like an app, without the address bar.
  3. In Chrome the Share button is in the address bar (top right); then choose Add to Home Screen the same way.
  4. Turn the device sideways.
  5. No sound? Tap the 🔇 Tap for sound badge (iPhone and iPad only play sound after a touch).

Android: Chrome → ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen works the same way.

Tablet or phone + controller

  1. Pair the controller over Bluetooth (device Settings → Bluetooth): Xbox: hold the small pair button on top until the Xbox button flashes fast. PlayStation: hold PS + Share/Create until the light bar flashes.
  2. Open the game and press any button on the controller.
  3. The touch buttons hide by themselves and the screen shows the controller's buttons. Touch the screen to bring them back.
  4. The buttons are the same as on a computer (see above). The screen stays on during a match.
