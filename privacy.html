PADEL LEGENDS · Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: Padel Legends does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no cross-site tracking.

Saved on your device

Your career, settings and tutorial progress are saved in your browser's local storage so the game remembers them. They stay on your device and are never sent to us. To remove them, delete your career in the game or clear this site's data in your browser.

The game also keeps its own files (models, sounds, fonts) in your browser's cache so it loads faster next time. Nothing personal is in them and they stay on your device; clearing this site's data removes them.

Visit counter

We use GoatCounter to count page visits and anonymous game events, such as "a match started" or "the tutorial was opened". GoatCounter sets no cookies and does not store your IP address. It uses it only briefly to tell visits apart. We see only totals (visits, country, browser, screen size, referring site), never individual players. Player names, room codes and anything you type are never sent. An ad blocker stops the counter, and the game works the same. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing roughly how many people play and which parts of the game they use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like every web server, it receives your IP address in order to deliver the pages, and GitHub may keep it in its server logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts and all game files come from this site, not from other services.

Online play

This site does not run an online game server yet. A server link (an address with ?server=) connects the game to a server that someone else runs, and the game asks you before it connects. That server receives:

  • your IP address (every server you connect to sees it),
  • the player you play with: name, look (body, hair, kit, racket and its colour), level and stats,
  • your equipment: racket, shoes, ball and the energy food in your bag,
  • the room code, the match settings you choose and your moves during the match.

Our game server gives each player a random rejoin code that is kept only in the open page, never saved. If your connection drops during a match, the game sends it back so you get your own seat again.

Padel Legends cannot see or control what such a server keeps, so only join servers of people you trust. The server address is taken out of the page address before the visit counter loads, so it is never counted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us what data about you we hold, and ask us to correct or delete it, to limit its use, or to stop using it. You can also complain to a data protection authority. The game keeps your data only on your own device, so you can see and delete it yourself (see "Saved on your device"). For anything else, write to contact@padellegends.eu.
