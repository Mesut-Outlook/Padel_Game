- Live lobby (plan 3, 2026-11-08):
  - **Server protocol:**
    - `join` with `quick: true` joins the longest-waiting open auto room of the same kind (`players`, doubles `cfg.mode` 'team' | 'face'), or opens a public one.
    - Team up rooms seat humans in [0, 1]; Face off rooms in [0, 2, 1, 3].
    - Auto rooms (`cfg.auto`, quick and invite) start `countdownMs` (5 s) after the second human joins. The lobby message carries `startsAt`.
    - `leave` gives the seat to the AI for good (no rejoin token) and sends the others `{ type: 'left', seat, name }`.
    - List rows are `{ code, players, mode, venue, free, host, char }`, taken from whoever holds the room now.
    - `cleanName` filters rude words, and `roomsPerIp` is 2.
    - Not adopted: requiring two different visitor keys before a match starts, because two people at one home share an IP.
  - **Client:**
    - `net.leave()` and `onLeft`.
    - `toMenu()` calls `leave`.
    - `playNow({ players, mode, invite, code })` in `main.js` uses the saved picks with no menus. While the room waits, a warm-up match vs AI runs offline, and the room's handle is in `pend`, not `net`. When the room starts, the online match takes over the scene (`world = null`, `avatars = []`, `setupScene` again, no reload).
    - The ✕ Exit button `#exitBtn` (agy-28) opens the reworked pause dialog "Leave the match?".
